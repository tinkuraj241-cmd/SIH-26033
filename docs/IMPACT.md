# Impact and Benefits — FarmConnect

## Intended beneficiaries

### Farmers and FPOs
- An additional channel to reach consumers and bulk buyers.
- Greater visibility of available produce and listed prices.
- An opportunity to receive buyer requirements and plan supply.

### Consumers
- Easier discovery of available local produce.
- Ability to compare seller listings and product details.
- A more direct way to communicate purchase requirements.

### Retailers and bulk buyers
- A structured channel to source produce from multiple farmers/FPOs.
- Better visibility of quantities and availability.
- Easier coordination of larger procurement requests.

### Logistics providers
- More organized pickup and delivery information.
- Potential to group nearby orders and reduce avoidable travel.

## Expected social, economic, and environmental benefits

- **Economic:** Potential improvement in market access and price transparency for producers, and more informed purchasing for buyers.
- **Social:** A digital channel that can include small-scale farmers and FPOs, subject to accessible design and onboarding support.
- **Operational:** Better coordination of orders, pickups, and deliveries.
- **Environmental:** Potential reduction in unnecessary trips through improved delivery planning; actual environmental benefit must be measured.

## Proposed impact indicators

| Area | Indicator |
|---|---|
| Farmer outcomes | Net realization per unit compared with a suitable local baseline |
| Buyer outcomes | Purchase price and product availability |
| Marketplace use | Active sellers, active buyers, repeat orders |
| Fulfilment | Order completion rate and time to fulfil |
| Logistics | Distance travelled per fulfilled order |
| Waste | Reported spoilage or unsold quantity |
| AI performance | Forecast error and route-planning performance |

## Measurement approach

A pilot should collect baseline data before launch and compare it with outcomes after use. Results should be reported with sample size, period, location, and methodology. Benefits listed above are expected outcomes, not measured achievements.

**Status:** Impact claims remain hypotheses until validated through a pilot.
