# Research and References — FarmConnect

The following resources are starting points for understanding agricultural markets, digital agriculture, mapping, and the proposed technology stack. They do not by themselves validate the proposed solution or establish measured project outcomes.

## Agriculture and market information

1. [AGMARKNET](https://agmarknet.gov.in/) — Agricultural market and price information. Relevant to understanding market-price data and possible data fields.
2. [e-NAM — National Agriculture Market](https://enam.gov.in/) — Information about India's electronic agricultural trading platform. Relevant background for digital agricultural market workflows.
3. [Farmers' Portal](https://farmer.gov.in/) — Agricultural information and services for farmers.
4. [Food and Agriculture Organization (FAO)](https://www.fao.org/) — Research and resources on agriculture, food systems, and rural development.

## Technical documentation

5. [Django documentation](https://docs.djangoproject.com/) — Reference for the proposed Python web backend.
6. [Pandas documentation](https://pandas.pydata.org/docs/) — Reference for tabular data processing.
7. [Scikit-learn documentation](https://scikit-learn.org/stable/) — Reference for baseline machine-learning and forecasting workflows.
8. [OpenStreetMap](https://www.openstreetmap.org/) — Open geographic data that may support mapping and route-planning features.
